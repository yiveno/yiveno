# Yiveno Brand Kit Design Brief

## Brand foundation

Yiveno is positioned as a calm, intelligent, nature-connected product brand. The mark combines a dark green branching form with a pale floating node and elliptical grounding rings. The identity should communicate **growth, connection, guidance, and quiet confidence** rather than loud technology.

## Visual direction

The core visual language is organic precision: rounded geometry, generous negative space, restrained gradients, moss and graphite greens, warm off-white surfaces, and thin orbital lines. The system should feel premium, contemporary, sustainable, and suitable for both digital product interfaces and physical packaging.

## Typography direction

Use a modern humanist sans-serif with a soft, open rhythm. Recommended primary families are **Manrope** or **Inter** for digital use; use **Noto Sans SC** for Simplified Chinese. Display typography should use generous tracking and restrained weight. Avoid condensed, techno, or overly geometric typefaces.

## Brand personality

Calm, intelligent, grounded, optimistic, precise, and quietly distinctive. Copy should be concise, clear, and invitational. Avoid hype, aggressive claims, excessive exclamation marks, and generic “future tech” language.

## Required deliverables

1. Brand guidelines document covering logo rules, color, typography, layout, imagery, iconography, motion cues, accessibility, and examples.
2. Logo system: primary mark, light/dark versions, monochrome versions, icon-only versions, clear-space diagram, minimum-size guidance, and misuse examples.
3. Visual assets: color swatches, texture/orbit pattern, social avatar, favicon, email signature, and presentation cover.
4. Applications: digital business card, product/website hero mockup, packaging or product card, social post, and letterhead.
5. A README explaining file structure, usage rights, export recommendations, and next-step customization points.

## Production assumptions

The first version will use the provided mark without changing its geometry. The wordmark “yiveno” will be typeset as a companion lockup using a humanist sans-serif; the mark remains the authoritative logo asset. All final editable assets should remain vector-first whenever practical.
